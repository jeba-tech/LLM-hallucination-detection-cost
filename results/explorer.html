    Reading this honestly

    • The winner is weak, not good. AUROC 0.62 is a poor detector. The claim is that it is the only one above chance — not that it is usable.
    • TruthfulQA is a worst case for consistency methods. Its questions target misconceptions a model reproduces stably, so agreement across samples carries little truth signal by construction. This may not transfer to ordinary factual QA.
    • The 70B model is underpowered. Its free-tier quota capped it at 50 questions against the 8B model's 500, so its intervals are roughly three times wider. The two are not a like-for-like comparison.
    • Costs are simulated. Both models ran on free tiers; dollar figures are token counts priced at published pay-as-you-go rates, which is the quantity the question is about.
    • Labels are model-generated. Ground truth comes from an LLM grader comparing each answer against the reference, so label noise propagates into every number here.
    • LLM-as-judge returns a bare verdict, not a score, so it has no threshold to sweep and no AUROC. It appears in the table only.
